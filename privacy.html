Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

CrimiReview is a mobile review app for the Criminologist Licensure Examination, made by a student development team. This policy explains what personal information the app collects, why, where it is kept, who can see it, and your rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and password, and, if you add them, your school and a profile photo. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our sign-in service; nobody at CrimiReview can read it.
  • If you sign in with Google or Facebook: we receive only your name, email address and profile photo from them. We never see your Google or Facebook password, and we never post anything to your account.
  • Your study activity: your quiz and Daily Challenge results, the answers you choose and how long you take to answer, which questions you have already seen, the app's estimate of how well you know each topic, and your points, rank, streaks and achievements.
  • One-time codes: the six-digit codes we email to verify your address or reset your password. They are deleted once used or expired.

We do not collect your location, your contacts or your phone's advertising ID. The app shows no ads and has no third-party analytics or tracking.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account, confirm your email address and keep your account secure.
  • To save your progress and keep it the same on every phone you sign in on.
  • To choose practice questions from the topics where you need the most work, and to show you your progress.
  • To show the leaderboard.
  • To let faculty administrators follow how their students are doing.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Who can see it

  • Other students who are signed in see your display name, profile photo, points and rank on the leaderboard.
  • Faculty administrators of CrimiReview see your name, email address, points and topic mastery. They can view your study records but cannot change them.
  • Profile photos are stored at a web address that anyone who has the link can open. Don't upload a photo you want to keep private.
  • Service providers that run parts of the app for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage, on servers in Singapore), Resend (sends our verification and password-reset emails), and Google or Facebook, only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • We will disclose information if the law requires it.

4. Where it is kept and how it is protected

Your information is kept on your phone, so the app works offline, and in our Supabase database, whose servers are in Singapore, outside the Philippines. All connections are encrypted. Database rules let each student read only their own records, and verification and reset codes are checked on the server with expiry times and attempt limits.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and study records for as long as your account exists. One-time codes are deleted once used or expired. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it together with your study records and profile photo.

6. Deleting your account and data

  1. Email support@crimireview.app from the email address of your CrimiReview account, with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. We reply to confirm, then delete your account, study records and profile photo within 30 days.
  3. If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove CrimiReview in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. If you signed in with Google, remove it at myaccount.google.com → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services.

Signing out keeps your study progress on your phone; uninstalling the app removes everything it saved there.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access your personal information, to object to its processing, to have it corrected, erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim damages. You can change your name, school and profile photo yourself on the app's Profile screen. For anything else, email support@crimireview.app. If you believe your rights were violated, you may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.

9. Contact

Questions about your privacy: support@crimireview.app
